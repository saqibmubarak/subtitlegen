# 5. References

Primary sources are preferred over roundup articles. Accessed during the August 2026 proposal research.

- **R1 — faster-whisper:** implementation, RTX 3070 Ti benchmarks, batching, and memory.  
  <https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper>

- **R2 — WhisperX:** faster-whisper batching, VAD, wav2vec2 forced alignment, and subtitle-oriented word timestamps.  
  <https://github.com/m-bain/whisperX>

- **R3 — Open ASR Leaderboard:** current public model comparisons.  
  <https://huggingface.co/spaces/hf-audio/open_asr_leaderboard>

- **R4 — Hugging Face leaderboard analysis:** short-form, multilingual, long-form, speed, and benchmark limitations.  
  <https://huggingface.co/blog/open-asr-leaderboard>

- **R5 — Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 model card:** languages, native timestamps, punctuation, and long-audio modes.  
  <https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3>

- **R6 — Canary-Qwen-2.5B model card:** English-only scope, architecture, speed, and input-length limits.  
  <https://huggingface.co/nvidia/canary-qwen-2.5b>

- **R7 — NeMo word boosting:** decoding-time phrase boosting for CTC, TDT, and AED models.  
  <https://docs.nvidia.com/nemo-framework/user-guide/latest/nemotoolkit/asr/asr_customization/word_boosting.html>

- **R8 — Fast Context-Biasing for CTC and Transducer ASR:** context graph and word-spotting approach.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07096>

- **R9 — LLM ASR correction with confidence filtering:** limits unnecessary transcript edits.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21414>

- **R10 — AnimeText:** large-scale anime scene-text dataset and evidence that document-trained detectors underperform on anime text.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07951>

- **R11 — manga-ocr:** recognition of vertical/horizontal Japanese, furigana, varied fonts, and text over images.  
  <https://github.com/kha-white/manga-ocr>

- **R12 — Direct Canary timestamp research:** timestamp-token method and reported timing errors; applies to released Canary timestamp work, not automatically to Canary-Qwen.  
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15646>
